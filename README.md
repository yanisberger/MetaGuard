# MetaGuard

Every photo taken on an iPhone silently stores metadata alongside the image itself. This includes GPS coordinates, device model, software version, unique identifiers, face detection data, and the physical orientation of the phone at the moment of capture. Most people are unaware this data exists, and sharing a photo shares all of it.

MetaGuard lets you inspect any photo from your camera roll and see exactly what it reveals about you. Each piece of metadata is explained in plain language with a risk assessment, so the privacy implications are concrete rather than abstract. You can then export a stripped copy of the photo with the sensitive metadata removed, safe to share anywhere.

The app runs entirely on-device. No data leaves your phone.
